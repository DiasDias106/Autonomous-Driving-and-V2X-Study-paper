\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This survey provided a comprehensive analysis of the Internet of Vehicles and V2X communication frameworks.
We examined the technical foundations separating cellular and dedicated short-range standards, evaluated the latency and reliability imperatives of cooperative perception, and highlighted privacy and security trade-offs within pseudonym systems.
Finally, we synthesized open challenges and outlined the migration path toward 6G-enabled connected vehicular ecosystems.
