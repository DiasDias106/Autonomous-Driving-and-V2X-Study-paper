\section{Challenges, Open Issues, and Future Directions}
\label{sec:challenges_future}

Deploying large-scale V2X ecosystems introduces multifaceted engineering and operational challenges.
Key open problems include resource allocation and congestion control in ultra-dense traffic scenarios, spectrum coexistence between DSRC and C-V2X variants, and seamless edge-computing offloading.
Looking ahead, the emergence of 6G-V2X networks promises integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), terahertz communications, and AI-native network management to support fully autonomous cooperative driving.
