\section{State of the Art: C-V2X vs. IEEE 802.11p/bd}
\label{sec:state_of_the_art}

A fundamental technological division in V2X communication exists between the IEEE 802.11 family of standards and 3GPP Cellular V2X (C-V2X) specifications \cite{naik2019ieee}.
Dedicated Short-Range Communications (DSRC), instantiated by IEEE 802.11p and modernized under IEEE 802.11bd, utilizes Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance (CSMA/CA) for distributed, ad-hoc medium access.
In contrast, C-V2X encompasses LTE-V2X (3GPP Releases 14 and 15) and 5G NR-V2X (Release 16 and beyond), introducing cellular-assisted resource allocation alongside autonomous sidelink scheduling (Mode 4 and Mode 2) \cite{sudirham2023sdn}.
Comparative evaluation across PHY/MAC layers, channel modulation, latency, throughput, and spectrum allocation in the 5.9~GHz ITS band reflects diverging design trade-offs and deployment trajectories globally.
