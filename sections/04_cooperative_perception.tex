\section{V2X for Cooperative Perception and Automated Driving}
\label{sec:cooperative_perception}

Cooperative perception empowers connected automated vehicles to share localized sensory information across vehicular networks, significantly extending situational awareness beyond individual visual horizons \cite{etsi_cpm_2019}.
By disseminating sensor observations via standard message formats, such as Collective Perception Messages (CPM), vehicles resolve occlusions and anticipate hidden hazards in complex traffic topologies.
However, supporting collaborative awareness and collective maneuvers introduces stringent Quality-of-Service (QoS) requirements.
These applications necessitate ultra-low communication latency (often strictly below 10~ms) and ultra-high packet delivery reliability exceeding 99.999\%.
Meeting these performance envelopes under heavy channel congestion and high vehicular mobility requires sophisticated sensor data fusion paradigms, adaptive message generation policies, and dynamic sidelink resource management.
