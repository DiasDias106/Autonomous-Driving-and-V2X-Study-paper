\section{V2X Security, Privacy, and Pseudonym Lifecycles}
\label{sec:security_privacy}

The integrity, authenticity, and non-repudiation of vehicular message exchanges are paramount to guaranteeing traffic safety, operational dependability, and user privacy.
Vehicular Public Key Infrastructure (V-PKI) frameworks address these imperatives through short-lived pseudonym certificates that conceal long-term vehicle identities.
Nevertheless, pseudonym lifecycle management—including certificate rollover, resolution, and revocation mechanisms—introduces substantial communication and storage overhead.
These constraints become particularly pronounced in high-density vehicular environments, motivating lightweight cryptographic primitives and decentralized trust architectures.
