\begin{abstract}
The rapid proliferation of connected and automated vehicles demands robust, ultra-reliable, and low-latency communication paradigms.
This survey investigates the integration of the Internet of Vehicles (IoV) and Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) communications as foundational pillars of modern intelligent transportation systems.
We provide an in-depth comparative analysis of Cellular V2X (C-V2X: LTE-V2X, 5G NR-V2X) and IEEE dedicated short-range communication standards (802.11p and 802.11bd), evaluating physical and MAC layer architectures, spectrum allocation, and global commercial deployment status.
Furthermore, we examine cooperative perception mechanisms, highlighting strict quality-of-service requirements for sensor sharing and collective maneuvers.
Finally, we discuss key architectural challenges, spectrum coexistence, edge intelligence integration, and the evolutionary pathway toward 6G-enabled vehicular networks.
\end{abstract}
